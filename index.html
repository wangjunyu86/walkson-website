About the Factory

Metal Fabrication in Chongqing's Industrial Belt

Walkson is located in Changshou District, Chongqing — next to the Chongqing steel industrial base, with a complete local supply chain for raw materials, subcontracting and logistics.

Since 2019 we have built a team with over 15 years of industry experience and eight nationally authorised patents, serving municipal, tourism and commercial projects worldwide.

  • Laser cutting, CNC bending and shearing equipment
  • Two production lines with 100% finished-goods inspection
  • Own logistics and installation team, 24/7 global response
  • Custom manufacturing to drawing, site conditions and budget
