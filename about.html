About Us

Chongqing Walkson Machinery Equipment Manufacturing Co., Ltd.

A metal fabrication manufacturer producing outdoor furniture, public shelters and municipal amenities for domestic and export markets since 2019.

Who We Are

Manufacturer & Trading Company

Walkson is based in Changshou District, Chongqing, close to the Chongqing steel industrial base. This location gives us a complete local supply chain for raw material sourcing, subcontract processing and logistics.

We specialise in customised metal products — integrating in-house R&D, custom production, global sales and full project support. Our core technical team brings more than 15 years of industry experience.

Our equipment includes laser cutting machines, CNC bending machines and shearing machines, supported by two production lines and a 100% finished-goods inspection process.

  • Founded 2019 · registered capital RMB 5 million
  • 1,564 m² manufacturing workshop
  • 8 nationally authorised patents
  • OEM and ODM services · customisation to sample or drawing
  • Exporting since May 2024 to North America, Europe, Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Africa and Oceania
